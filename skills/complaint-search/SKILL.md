---
name: complaint-search
description: Run a product search that stops before a name. Use when asked for a viral product, a gap, or an industry-changing idea.
---

Run three agents in order. Do not skip a stage.

1. job-source. Ten quoted jobs, workaround, frequency, link. No fix.
2. gap-cut. One line each: tool they use, thing it does not do. Delete the rest.
3. pairing-maker. Three pairings, no shared verb. One text sentence. Stop.

Do not aim at "transform the industry." That phrase is banned as an input. A config cannot guarantee a hit. The human test is the only pass: 3 of 5 do the action with no help, and 1 does it again.

Do not use gan-planner for this search. It names an app and expands features. That is a later step, after the number is hit.
